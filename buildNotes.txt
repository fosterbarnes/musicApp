v0.3.5 profesionalTugboat [fix > crossfade, queue; feat. > rebind keys]

- Crossfades are smoother and now work between mono and stereo songs and when Repeat All loops back to the start
- Repeat One now stays on the same song when crossfade is on
- Seeking during a crossfade now works, and the player switches to the new song as soon as the fade starts
- Pausing in WASAPI exclusive mode is now silent instead of repeating a short piece of the song or buzzing
- Pause and resume fade very briefly, so they no longer click
- Starting shuffle from the middle of a list now includes the whole list
- Editing the queue no longer changes the order of your library
- Songs added to your library during playback no longer join the current queue
- Songs with identical tags, like several "Unknown Title" files, are no longer mixed up in the queue
- A queued song loses its queued highlight once it starts playing
- Undo no longer brings back songs you removed from the library
- Play counts no longer go up when you restart a song or skip through songs while paused
- Deleting or externally editing the next song in the queue no longer gets blocked for most of the current song
- Right-click a song in the Queue, queue popup, or miniplayer queue and choose Remove from Queue
- New shortcuts: Ctrl+Q adds selected songs to the queue and Shift+Enter plays them next
- In the Queue, Delete removes the selected song and Alt+Up/Alt+Down move it
- Ctrl+S toggles shuffle, Ctrl+R changes repeat mode, Ctrl+Up/Ctrl+Down change volume, and M mutes
- Every shortcut can be changed in Settings > Shortcuts, and the table is cleaner and easier to read
