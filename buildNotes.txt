v0.3.6 cyberbussyKimmie [fix > settings, library, queue, updater]

- Column widths, shuffle, repeat and volume no longer reset after moving the window or changing other settings.
- Maximize now fits the screen at any display scaling and stays on the monitor the window is on, and the window no longer opens off-screen after a monitor is unplugged.
- Muting no longer brings the app back silent after a restart.
- A folder you can't read no longer stops a library scan, and files deleted during a scan no longer break it.
- Removing a song while a scan finishes no longer brings it back, and the Playlists pane no longer blanks during scans.
- Library, playlist and folder changes are no longer lost when two saves overlap.
- Long tracks show their full length, and plays or favorites made during a rescan are kept.
- Songs removed from imported playlists stay removed, and imported .m3u playlists with accented names in older formats now match your songs.
- "Move to Recycle Bin" never deletes a file permanently.
- Removing or deleting the playing song keeps the rest of your queue.
- You can drag a song to the very end of the queue, and holding Alt+Up/Down keeps moving it.
- Double-clicking in Recently Played plays that list once instead of the whole library or twice.
- Songs that end before their crossfade now fade the next song in instead of starting abruptly.
- Saving tags or artwork on the next song near the end of the current one no longer fails with "file in use".
- In the miniplayer and queue popup, right-click actions now work with several songs selected.
- Searches like "AC/DC" or "Don't" now find results, and typing the word "Search" works.
- Album art prefers the front cover, Picard-tagged MusicBrainz ids are used, and iTunes artwork is found.
- Editing lyrics in an outside editor no longer wipes them or repeats error popups.
- Closing Settings stops a running missing-artwork scan, and a sidebar set to exactly 250 px stays that width.
- If two shortcuts end up on the same key, the later one goes back to its default instead of silently doing nothing.
- Recently Played's right-click menu now includes Show in Albums.
- The column resize handle is wider and centered on the divider, so it is easier to grab.
- The updater shows an error on slow connections instead of silently closing, can't be closed mid-install, and puts the old app back if a portable update fails.
