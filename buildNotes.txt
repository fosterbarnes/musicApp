v0.3.4 sandiegoTaters [feat. > miniplayer volume; fix > lost miniplayer]

- Miniplayer has a volume slider at the bottom of the album art that fades in on hover and stays in sync with the main window
- Right-click the top track in the miniplayer queue and choose Swap with Current to play it right away in place of the current song
- The miniplayer no longer opens invisibly off the edge of the screen on multi-monitor setups with display scaling. If it is lost now, it comes back the next time you open it
- The time bar fill now matches the time shown after the window is snapped, maximized, or resized by another app
- Fix 'Add to Queue' not working if there's no running queue
- Re-scanning library no longer resets 'recently added' dates play count, or favorite status
- Miniplayer artist & album are clickable
- Fix issue where selecting "Go to album" wouldn't always place the item on screen
- Add undo button to miniplayer for when the queue gets reset. Restores previous queue state
- Play errors or missing files no longer wipe the whole queue
- Mouse wheel & trackpad scrolling speed dialed down
- Fix 'excessive loading' on app startup for lack of a better term
